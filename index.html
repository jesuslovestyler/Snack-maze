How to play

Guide the little yellow muncher through the maze and eat every snack dot to clear the level. Eat all the dots, dodge the Grumps, and chase the bonuses.

  • Power apples (one glowing in each corner) turn every Grump dark blue and scared for about 7 seconds. Gobble a fleeing Grump for 200, then 400, 800, and 1600 points in a chain.
  • A golden peach worth 500 points appears under the Grump pen partway through each level — grab it before it vanishes.
  • Touching a Grump in its normal colors costs a life. You have 3 lives; each new level refills the dots and everything moves a little faster.

Meet the Grumps:

  • Blitz (red) — charges straight at you.
  • Sneak (teal) — sneaky: aims for the spot just ahead of you.
  • Wander (green) — roams the maze wherever it pleases.
  • Shy (pink) — comes after you, but loses its nerve and backs off when it gets close.
