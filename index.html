How to play

Guide the little turtle through the garden maze and eat every berry to clear the level. Eat all the berries, dodge the slugs, and chase the bonuses.

  • Strawberries (one glowing in each corner) turn every slug pale and scared for about 7 seconds. Gobble a fleeing slug for 200, then 400, 800, and 1600 points in a chain.
  • A golden peach worth 500 points appears under the slug pen partway through each level — grab it before it vanishes.
  • Touching a slug in its normal colors costs a life. You have 3 lives; each new level refills the berries and everything moves a little faster.

Meet the slugs:

  • Blitz (orange-red) — charges straight at you.
  • Sneak (teal) — sneaky: aims for the spot just ahead of you.
  • Wander (green) — roams the garden wherever it pleases.
  • Shy (pink) — comes after you, but loses its nerve and backs off when it gets close.
